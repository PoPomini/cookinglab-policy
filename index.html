개인정보처리방침

시행일: 2026년 11월 11일

CookingLab(이하 "게임")은 PoPomini(이하 "개발자")가 만든 모바일 게임입니다. 이 방침은 게임과 관련해 어떤 정보를 왜, 얼마나 오래 다루는지 쉽게 설명합니다.

1. 기본 원칙

  • 게임에는 회원 가입이나 로그인이 없습니다. 게임 안에서 이름·이메일·전화번호를 입력받지 않습니다.
  • 다만 이용자가 직접 문의 메일을 보내거나, 비공개 테스트 참여자가 직접 테스트 기록을 보내면 아래 2항의 정보를 받습니다.
  • 게임 진행(발견한 요리, 코인, 설정 등)은 기기 안에만 저장되며 개발자에게 전송되지 않아, 개발자는 이 정보를 보유하지 않습니다. 기기에서 Android 백업(Google 계정 자동 백업)을 켜 두었다면 Android가 이 데이터를 이용자의 Google 계정에 백업할 수 있습니다. 이 백업은 Google이 관리하며 개발자는 볼 수 없습니다.

2. 처리하는 개인정보의 목적·항목·보유기간

  • 문의 메일: 설정의 '문의하기'를 누르면 메일 앱이 열리고 본문에 게임 버전·기기 모델·OS·언어가 자동으로 적힙니다. 보내기 전에 이용자가 지울 수 있습니다.
  • 테스트 기록: 출시 전 테스트 버전에만 있는 기능입니다. 이용자가 설정에서 동의하고 '테스트 기록 보내기'를 직접 누를 때만 보내며, 자동으로 보내지 않습니다. 기기 고유 ID나 광고 ID는 보내지 않고, 닉네임은 실명이 아니어도 됩니다. 출시 버전에는 이 기능이 없습니다.

개발자는 개인정보를 제3자에게 제공하지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

CookingLab (the "Game") is a mobile game made by PoPomini (the "Developer"). This policy explains, in plain words, what information is handled in connection with the Game, why, and for how long.

1. Basics

  • The Game has no sign-up or login. The Game never asks you to enter your name, email or phone number.
  • However, if you send us a support email, or if you are a closed-test participant and choose to send test records, we receive the information in section 2.
  • Your progress (discovered dishes, coins, settings, etc.) is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Developer, so the Developer does not hold it. If Android backup (automatic backup to your Google Account) is on, Android may back up this data to your Google Account. Google manages that backup; the Developer cannot see it.

  • Test records: this feature exists only in pre-release test builds. 6. Other external services

  • In-app review: the Game may ask for a rating using Google Play's in-app review feature. Google Play handles it; the Developer does not receive who left which review through this feature.
  • Discord: the community button in Settings opens Discord, an external service. Your activity there is covered by Discord's privacy policy.
  • This policy page: hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you open it, access logs such as your IP address may be recorded by GitHub.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: in the release version, the Developer does not collect crash reports or usage analytics. If such a feature is added, this policy will be updated first.

7. How data is deleted

When the retention period ends or we receive a deletion request, we promptly delete the emails and records (electronic files) so they cannot be recovered. Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game or tap "Reset Game" in Settings.

8. Your rights (and those of legal guardians)

  • You can ask at any time to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information.
  • Send your request to the email in section 10 (for test records, include your nickname). We will reply within 10 days of receiving it.
  • Legal guardians of children under 14 have the same rights.

9. Security measures

  • Only the Developer can access the information received.
  • The Google Account storing it uses 2-step verification.
  • We receive only the minimum information needed.

12. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

13. Rights of users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

  • You can change or withdraw your ad consent at any time with the "Privacy Options" button in Settings (shown only in regions where consent is required).
  • You can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • Legal bases: ads = consent (UMP) or legitimate interests (non-personalised ads); support email = legitimate interests (answering you); test records = consent.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and important changes will also be announced in game update notes.
